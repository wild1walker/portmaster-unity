Unity Port Prepatcher

For ports made with portmaster-unity. Give it the port's zip and your copy of the game. In your browser it unpacks the game and converts its textures, the longest part of the setup on a handheld, with the same code the handheld uses, on every core of your computer. The zip it writes has the game where the port's setup looks for it, so the first launch on the handheld only does the rest. Nothing leaves your computer.
